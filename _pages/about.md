<p class="research-tagline" data-en="Artificial intelligence for understanding the human brain" data-zh="用人工智能理解人脑">Artificial intelligence for understanding the human brain</p>

<p data-i18n="aboutP1">I am an undergraduate student in Electronic Information Engineering at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC). My research interests center on applying deep learning to neuroimaging, with a particular focus on white matter structure and brain disease analysis.</p>

<p data-i18n="aboutP2">My work spans streamline-level tractography modeling, multimodal MR–ultrasound registration, and 3D anatomical landmark detection. I am interested in representations that make complex imaging data useful for both prediction and interpretation.</p>

<h2 data-i18n="researchTitle">Research focus</h2>
<ul class="focus-list">
<li><strong data-i18n="r1title">Brain connectivity</strong><span data-i18n="r1desc">Modeling individual white matter streamlines with graph networks and multi-scale transformers to study population differences and brain disease.</span></li>
<li><strong data-i18n="r2title">Multimodal alignment</strong><span data-i18n="r2desc">Bridging MR and ultrasound images through style transfer and coarse-to-fine registration for brain imaging applications.</span></li>
<li><strong data-i18n="r3title">3D medical understanding</strong><span data-i18n="r3desc">Combining 2D image learning with volumetric depth recovery to localize anatomical landmarks in clinical CT scans.</span></li>
</ul>

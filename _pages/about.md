I am an undergraduate student in Electronic Information Engineering at the [University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC)](https://www.uestc.edu.cn/).

My research interests lie in deep learning for brain imaging, particularly diffusion MRI and white matter tractography. I have also worked on MR–ultrasound image registration and 3D anatomical landmark detection in CT.

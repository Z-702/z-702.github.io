<p data-en="My research focuses on learning useful representations from complex medical images, with brain imaging as the central application." data-zh="我关注从复杂医学影像中学习有效的数据表示，并将脑影像分析作为主要应用方向。">My research focuses on learning useful representations from complex medical images, with brain imaging as the central application.</p>

<h2 data-en="White matter tractography" data-zh="脑白质纤维分析">White matter tractography</h2>
<p data-i18n="p1desc">A parcellation-free framework that turns individual white matter streamlines into multi-scale representations for group classification and attention-based interpretation.</p>
<p><strong>TractoMFormer</strong> · DGCNN · Multi-scale Vision Transformer · dMRI</p>
<p><a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2026.121992">NeuroImage paper ↗</a> · <a href="https://github.com/Z-702/mvit_PPMI">GitHub project ↗</a></p>

<h2 data-en="MR–ultrasound registration" data-zh="磁共振与超声影像配准">MR–ultrasound registration</h2>
<p data-i18n="p2desc">A coarse-to-fine pipeline combining 3D CycleGAN style transfer, affine registration, and local deformation to align pre-surgery MR and post-resection ultrasound images.</p>
<p><strong>ReMIND2Reg / Learn2Reg 2024</strong> · CycleGAN · SynthMorph</p>
<p><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05240">Preprint ↗</a></p>

<p data-i18n="p3desc">A projection-based framework combining HRNet landmark localization with constrained 3D depth recovery for dense facial landmark detection in CT.</p>
<p><strong>CT</strong> · HRNet · 2D-to-3D depth recovery</p>

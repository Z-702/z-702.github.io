My research focuses on deep learning methods for brain and medical image analysis.

## White matter tractography

In TractoMFormer, we represent individual white matter streamlines using a graph network and analyze the resulting representations with a multi-scale vision transformer for group classification.

[Paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuroimage.2026.121992) · [Code](https://github.com/Z-702/mvit_PPMI) · [Summary]({{ '/publication/tractomformer/' | relative_url }})

## MR–ultrasound registration

This work uses image style transfer and coarse-to-fine registration to align pre-surgery MR with post-resection ultrasound images.

[Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05240) · [Summary]({{ '/publication/mr-ultrasound-registration/' | relative_url }})

## 3D anatomical landmark detection

This submitted manuscript studies facial landmark localization in CT using 2D HRNet predictions followed by volumetric depth recovery.
